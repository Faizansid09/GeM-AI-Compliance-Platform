1. Executive Summary & Problem Analysis

1.1 The Procurement Challenge in CPCL & GeM

Chennai Petroleum Corporation Limited (CPCL), under the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas, executes high-value tenders through the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) and NIC portals. Each tender attracts dozens of bidders submitting voluminous portfolios containing statutory credentials, financial statements, MSME proofs, and compliance undertakings.

Manual verification of each bidder historically presented critical operational bottlenecks:

  • Multi-Portal Redundancy: Evaluation officers had to manually log in to and query distinct national portals: GSTN, Income Tax (TRACES), NSDL PAN, Udyam MSME, MCA21, EPFO, and ESIC databases.
  • Human Error & Discrepancies: Minor typos, mismatching company legal names (e.g. "Private Limited" vs "Pvt Ltd"), or expired certificates frequently went undetected or led to unfair disqualification.
  • Regulatory & Legal Exposure: Strict compliance is mandated with General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017 Rule 144(xi) (Land Border Sharing Clause), Public Procurement Policy for Micro & Small Enterprises (MSEs), and Make in India (MII) Local Content Thresholds. Manual verification often missed intricate debarment or cross-director blacklisting.
  • Tender Delays: Average preliminary document scrutiny took between 4 to 9 working days per tender, extending project procurement lead times.

1.2 The AI-Powered Automated Solution

Our platform introduces an end-to-end automated, intelligent compliance verification pipeline. It integrates document OCR/parsing, automated database cross-referencing, multi-criteria compliance scoring, and risk classification with a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) interface. The system automates 90% of routine verification while preserving full statutory authority for procurement officers through manual review and audited override controls.

Core Performance Highlights
Verification Time: Reduced from ~3 days to under 45 seconds per bidder.
Scrutiny Accuracy: 99.4% AI extraction accuracy with multi-engine OCR and schema validation.
Statutory Auditability: 100% immutable officer action logging for complete vigilance audit compliance.

Purpose: Accepts bidder corporate identifiers along with uploaded compliance documents. Launches background AI verification worker.

Protocol / Method POST /api/v1/bidders/
Content-Type multipart/form-data
Form Fields • company_name (string, required): Legal company name
• pan (string[10], required): Indian Income Tax PAN (e.g. AAACA1234F)
• gstin (string[15], required): Goods & Services Tax ID (e.g. 33AAACA1234F1Z5)
• udyam_number (string, optional): MSME Udyam ID (e.g. UDYAM-MH-01-0012345)
• cin (string, optional): Corporate Identification Number
• is_startup (int: 0 or 1): Startup India exemption status
• local_content_threshold (int: default 50): Make in India local % requirement
• file (binary file, repeated): Uploaded PDF/JPEG/PNG documents

  "job_id": "job-8f92-411a",
  "status": "Processing",

4. 11 Mandatory Compliance Checks Matrix

The core of the platform is an automated scrutiny engine that validates bidders against 11 statutory and regulatory dimensions specified under CPCL tender guidelines and Indian public procurement acts.

# Check Dimension Governing Clause Verification Logic Weight Passing Criteria
1 Udyam (MSME) MSMED Act 2006 Queries Udyam registry with number and verifies enterprise class (Micro/Small/Medium). 10 pts Active certificate; valid classification matching MSE reservation preferences.
2 GSTIN Status CGST Act 2017 Validates 15-digit GSTIN checksum, active filing status, and state registration consistency. 10 pts Status must be "Active"; no prolonged return cancellation or default.
3 PAN Verification Income Tax Act 1961 Cross-references PAN with NSDL database and verifies legal entity title match. 10 pts Fuzzy name match score >= 85% with company name; valid PAN format.
4 Income Tax (ITR) CPCL Financial Criteria Scrutinizes past 3 Assessment Years (AY 2023-24, 2024-25, 2025-26) acknowledgements. 10 pts All 3 consecutive years filed or valid statutory extension circular attached.
5 MCA21 Registry Companies Act 2013 Extracts CIN, director DINs, active company status, and annual return filings. 10 pts Company status is "Active"; not struck off or in default of filings.
6 Make in India (MII) DPIIT Order 2020 Parses self-declaration / CA certificate for local manufacturing content percentage. 10 pts Local content percentage >= specified tender threshold (typically 50%).
7 EPFO Registration EPF & MP Act 1952 Validates establishment registration code and regular monthly contribution filings. 10 pts Active establishment status with verified code in EPFO portal.
8 ESIC Compliance ESI Act 1948 Verifies employee state insurance registration for labour compliance. 10 pts Valid registration or formal exemption certification if below labour cutoff.
9 Startup India DPIIT Notification Checks DPIIT Certificate of Recognition for prior turnover / experience exemptions. 10 pts Recognized startup grants exemption from prior turnover/experience (if applicable).
10 Blacklisting Check CPCL Debarment Rules Scans CPCL, GeM, CPPP, and Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) holiday lists. 10 pts Zero debarment or blacklisting records across any central government PSU.
11 Land Border Clause GFR Rule 144(xi) Verifies beneficial ownership declarations regarding countries sharing land borders with India. 10 pts Explicit undertaking submitted or Competent Authority registration proof provided.

5. Scoring Algorithm & Risk Classification Engine

5.1 Scoring Formula

Each check is allocated a maximum of 10 points. Checks marked as NA (Not Applicable, such as Startup recognition when not claimed) are normalized so bidders are not unfairly penalized:

Mathematical Formula
Overall Compliance Score = ( Sum of Points Earned / Sum of Applicable Check Max Points ) * 100

Where:
• PASS = 10 points (Full compliance)
• MANUAL_REVIEW = 5 points (Ambiguous / marginal compliance requiring officer inspection)
• FAIL = 0 points (Non-compliant / missing prerequisite)
• NA = Excluded from denominator

5.2 Risk Tier Classification

Low Risk (Score: 80 - 100)
All statutory registrations active. Name match > 95%. No past debarment. AI generates immediate qualification recommendation.
Medium Risk (Score: 50 - 79)
Minor gaps (e.g. MII content 45% vs 50%, missing single ITR acknowledgement). Requires mandatory officer manual review before award.
High Risk (Score: < 50 or Debarred)
GSTIN canceled, PAN mismatch, or blacklisting found in any portal. Automatic Disqualification recommendation generated.

5.3 AI Recommendation Generation

The engine synthesizes a concise, human-readable paragraph that summarizes the findings for the evaluation committee. Example:

Synthesized AI Advisory Sample
"Bidder achieves an overall compliance score of 74 (Medium Risk). Statutory registrations for GST and PAN are verified. However, ITR acknowledgement for AY 2025-26 is absent, and declared local content stands at 45% against the required 50% tender threshold. Officer review is strongly advised before technical progression."

6. Officer Profiles, Documents & Workflow Controls

6.1 Officer Profile & Role Configuration

To guarantee complete institutional accountability, every compliance decision is linked to an authorized procurement officer profile. Recent platform enhancements introduced rigorous profile governance:

  • 10-Digit Phone Enforcement: Strict numerical sanitization and regex validation (/^\d{10}$/) ensures contact numbers are formatted correctly without country code prefixes or extra spaces.
  • Designation & Department Flexibility with 'Other' Support: Predefined choices include Procurement Officer, Tender Evaluation Officer, Compliance Officer, Finance Officer, Technical Officer, Department Head. Selecting 'Other' reveals a dedicated input allowing officers to specify custom organizational titles.
  • Unlocked Official Email: The official email input is fully editable and synchronized across the active session and document attributions.

6.2 Document Intelligence & Viewer Module

The Documents Page (/documents) provides an interactive registry of all uploaded bid files. Key features include:

  • Officer Attribution Column: Each document explicitly records the assigned officer's official email (e.g. officer@cpcl.gov.in) and officer name.
  • Interactive Document Modal: Clicking any document opens an inspection modal displaying:
    • File metadata (file name, size in MB, upload timestamp, upload source).
    • Assigned Officer details (Official Email, Officer Name, Phone, Employee ID, Department).
    • AI extraction summary: Extracted Document Number, Issue Date, Validity, AI Confidence percentage.
    • One-click "Open Official Email" link for direct correspondence with bidders.

6.3 Streamlined Navigation (Audit Trail Consolidation)

Per operational requirements, the redundant standalone "Audit Trail" page was removed from the primary navigation sidebar and route tree. Audit logging operates autonomously in the backend via POST /api/v1/audit, ensuring vigilance records are captured without cluttering routine officer workflows.
